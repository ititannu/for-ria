01 · the main message

Okay so.... hi.

🌸

This is for the girl who was once my home in human form. I don't really know how to write this, or if I'll ever actually give this to you. But I think I still have a lot of things left to say, and maybe writing them down is easier than trying to say them out loud, ig.

You became such a huge part of my life so naturally that I don't even remember when you stopped being just a friend and started feeling like family.

And since your birthday was coming up, I knew I wanted to do something for you. I didn't really know what, or if you'd even want it, but I still wanted to put in the effort and make something that was actually from me to you. So... I made you this little corner of the internet.

Maybe you'll read everything here, maybe you won't. Maybe you'll keep it, maybe you'll delete it. That's completely up to you. I just wanted to be able to say that, for your birthday, I did something with all the things I never really knew how to say.

Whatever happens after this, at least I'll know I tried. And maybe that's enough.

— always, in some corner of my heart
02 · a letter, half hindi half english — like we always talked

Ek lambi si chitthi

💗for
Ria

Ab mujhe genuinely nahi pata kya likhu, because there is just SO much. So I guess I'll just start wherever my brain takes me. I have a LOT to say — before all the serious stuff, I want to remember us the way I actually remember us: being absolute idiots together.

us being idiots (the good years)

Jab hum 2024 me mille thhe kuch logo ke waje se jinko baad me jaake humne ditch krdia tha T-T, then hum saath rhne lag gye thhe. Ysp pe toh aadhe logo se mein tere waje se hi mili thi. Times were good back then — raat bhar chatting krna, vc me rhna, full on yapping. Koi itna jada busy bhi nai tha jitna ab hogye hai sab.

Tum sab Minecraft khelte and mein bas dekhti thi, my network sucks bro, same with roblox, ugh. And yess, insta pe hopeless romantic reels ek dusre ko bhejna jab tera school crush tha XD.

Woh week yaad hai jisme tunne record toda tha — 24 hrs vc, bilkul soyi nai thi, vc and chat stats me top, ek dam "NO.1 vella" ka tag, lmao. Phir baad me aayu and zeus aaye, in dono ke kitne majhe lete thhe, bromance gay couple bna diya tha, raat ko bak bak krna, peace bhi hota tha.

Saal ke end me meri aur aayu ki ladai hogyi thi toh tunen bola tha chance dede, blah blah shit, zeus ka dimaag kharab kiya tha humne toh ki usne insta grp hi bna diya, he was like "jo baat krni hai yaha kro for god's sake." Phir dusre din tujhe add krdia tha, peace bhi aagya usme, raat ko ludo khelna, reels bhejna, bahutt majhaa aata tha. And idk kya hogya, pura grp tut gya T-T. I still miss those daysss yaaarrr.

the day you actually came to see me

Sabse main chiz, you actually came to meet me. Like, an ONLINE FRIEND literally showed up in real life and you even bunked clg for it. I don't think I'll ever forget that bro — yeah meetup thoda sa boring tha kyuki hm jada baat nai kiye thhe, butt yess humne burger khaya tha, teri pic mere pass abhi bhii haiii saved. Plus again, thank youu, tumne mujhe pendant diya tha and bracelet, and ykww mein abhi bhi pehenti hu, maine utara nai hai, mein khud se jada safe rakhti — isko tutne hi nai denaa hai, iske waje se lgta hai ki tu hai abhi bhi mere saath. But regret bhi hota hai ki maine kuch diya nai tujhe, sorry. I wish I had something to give you that day too.

about our breakup...

Now let's talk about our breakup. And ig I can't talk about us without talking about what happened either. I know I wasn't completely right. There are things I did that I wish I'd handled differently, and I genuinely take responsibility for those. I also know that there were things on your side that hurt me too. I'm not writing this to decide who was right or wrong, because I don't think either of us came out of it completely innocent.

Maine immaturity ki thi last year and 2026 ke starting months me, ik that, aur humara end me patch up ho bhi hogya tha, bhale hi pehle jaisa naa ho relation — but still, I fucked up bro. Mein khud bolri hu ki maine kuch logo ko bola ki tu ignore krri hai hume, tu bas peace ke saath baate krti hai — ab ye woh, genuinely nai pta tha ki tu busy hai ya teri life me kuch chalra hai, ye nai socha humne, chalo MAINE nai samjha, ye bolti hu. I thought you're ignoring us on purpose, you're not busy and just making excuses, sorry for that. Maine teri backbitching ki thi patchup hone se pehle jo tujhe pta, sorry uske liye, phir jo misunderstanding me bola uske liye bhi maafi. I know ab sorry bolne se kuch magically theek nhi hone wala. Tu hurt hai, and mein bhi hurt hu.

But I still wanted to acknowledge where I was wrong instead of pretending I wasn't. And haan, I can genuinely say ki main pehle se bahut better ho gayi hoon. Guarantee nahi de rahi ki main ab full gyaani insaan ban gayi hu okay na, but thodi maturity aur akal aa gyi hai mujhme. I'm not the same person I was back then, just know that — mein jitna bhi cool ban jau sabke saamne, but I still miss you everyday, like mera 1000 speed me crashout hota hai, neend nai aati 5 baje se pehle. Tujhse milne ka bhi mann krta hai, ab toh mein bhi school bunk krdeti hu, 5-6 baar krliya maine. If we were still together as friends, I would've probably just come to you and spent time with you, the way you came all the way to meet me. T-T

Meri toh mummy bhi puchti hai "ria se baat nai hoti kya ab teri", mein bahaana bnaa deti hu, because I can't bring myself to tell her that we had a breakup. That's it — maine last year bhi tujhe chota sa b'day gift diya tha jo jada accha nai tha, isliye is baar better bnaa rhi hu, jitna mein kr skti.

for now...

I just hope life is always kind to you. I hope you find happiness in the little things, people who genuinely care about you, and the kind of peace you've always deserved. And even if I'm not there to see it, I hope you're happy.

"And ho sake toh cake thoda jada kha lena mere taraf se."

Happy birthday, Ria. 🤍
